Encryption in transit

How the layers work together

No single control carries the load. Each layer covers something the others don’t.

  1. Encryption

    TLS 1.2 and 1.3 protect data moving between components, and encryption protects stored data.

  2. Server identity

    Certificate validation checks who is on the other end of each connection.

  3. Network path

    Runners call out on port 443 only, and PrivateLink can keep traffic off the public internet.

  4. Secrets

    Only the runner can turn a secret reference into a value, and AI features never receive one.

  5. Access

    Single sign-on, MFA and roles decide who can start each request, and Maia can’t exceed the user’s access.

  6. Audit

    The audit service records who did what and when, so every request leaves a trail.

What reaches the AI

What reaches the model, and what never does

✓

What reaches the model

Prompts and replies, table and column names, pipeline definitions and context files. Also a bounded sample of rows, up to 200, when sampling is on. You can switch sampling off so Maia works from metadata only.

✕

What never does

Secret values, configured environment values and bulk copies of your warehouse. Nothing sent is used to train, fine-tune or benchmark any model.

Maia proposes, people decide

Levels of oversight

Maia has no standing privilege of its own. It acts as the person who invoked it. How much Maia does before a person steps in.

  1. 1

    Assisted

    The default. Maia proposes a change and a person reviews and commits it.

  2. 2

    Supervised

    Maia asks first before sampling data, running a query, executing a pipeline or deleting an object.

  3. 3

    Customer-configured automation

    Opt-in only. Maia can’t switch it on, and it runs within the task identity’s permissions.

Maia proposes, people decide

Layers of access control

Three switches that decide who can use Maia and what it can do.

  1. 1

    Account

    Switch Maia off for the whole account, which also works as an emergency stop.

  2. 2

    User

    Grant or withhold Maia for each user as part of their role.

  3. 3

    Action

    Explicit approval for anything that reads data or changes state.

Maia proposes, people decide

Built-in safeguards

Guardrails built into how Maia works.

  1. Inherited permissions

    Maia can’t exceed the access of the person who invoked it.

  2. One-way secrets

    Maia can use a secret through a runner, but never read it.

  3. Nothing auto-commits

    Changes are held until you commit them, and you can interrupt Maia at any point.

Certifications and standards

Maia sits inside Matillion’s SOC 2 and ISO 27001 boundary, and Matillion documents alignment with GDPR and HIPAA. AI controls are mapped to the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

Testing

An annual penetration test, with the summary available in the Trust Center, plus a continuous bug bounty.

Retention

Conversation history is kept for two years, observability traces for 30 days, and the model provider retains no prompts or completions.
