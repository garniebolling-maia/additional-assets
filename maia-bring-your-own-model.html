Sampling control

Maia asks before it samples data, and an account-level setting turns sampling off. With it off, only metadata goes to the model.

Updates

An auto-update option keeps the platform current, and updating a runner means restarting it.

Network and certificates

The runner sits in your network and needs outbound access to the hub. Custom certificates are set through runner environment variables.

The Matillion Trust Center holds the white paper on deployment and what Maia Team sends to the model. Use it as the reference for your security review.

Architecture

Hybrid architecture: how data flows

Maia manages the control plane. Your runner does the work, next to your data.

1 Trigger

You build, schedule or run a pipeline in Maia.

2 Instructions

Maia sends the pipeline instructions to your runner over TLS. The runner connects outbound to the hub.

3 Execution

The runner reads and writes your data sources directly, so data processing stays in your environment.

4 Status

Run status and metadata return to Maia for monitoring, audit and history.

This shows the hybrid deployment. A full SaaS option also exists. The Trust Center white paper and architecture diagrams are the reference for your security review.

Connecting your model

Connect your model for operations

The Matillion MCP server lets an approved AI client work with your Maia resources.

What it does

Monitor pipeline executions, analyze credit consumption and trigger pipeline runs from your assistant.

Safe by default

Tools are read-only unless you turn on write tools, so you decide what the client can change.

Works in your IDE

Set it up in VS Code or Claude Code with an mcp.json file and your credentials.

MCP is for operating and monitoring. Build instructions arrive as an exported plan.

How they interact

Your model and Maia, side by side

Two separate paths: operating Maia through MCP, and building from a plan you export.

1 Plan

Your model and RAG write the plan in your IDE, using your standards.

2 Hand off

Export it as Markdown and upload it, or copy and paste it into Maia Team. Maia builds, and a person approves.

3 Operate

Your client talks to Maia through the MCP server. Tools are read-only unless you enable write tools.

4 Results

Run status, failures, credit use and audit events come back to your assistant.

Moving over

Starting from what you have

From Matillion ETL

The Upgrade Assistant imports jobs into Maia Foundation. A migration analysis reports, component by component, what migrates as is and what needs revalidation. In assessments our team has run, 90% or more typically migrates directly.

From other tools

Maia Team can read an exported job definition, such as a JSON file, and recommend a plan to convert it, including efficiency improvements.

Your Git

Maia works with GitHub, Azure, GitLab and Bitbucket, or you can use Maia-hosted Git. Approval to connect your repository is the step to start early.
